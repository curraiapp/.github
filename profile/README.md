### What is Currai?

Currai helps product and engineering teams understand how their AI agents
perform in real conversations.

- **Understand user needs.** Discover intents, recurring requests, and friction.
- **Find agent failures.** Identify errors and violations of the rules you define.
- **Follow the evidence.** Investigate conversations alongside model and tool traces.
- **Improve with context.** Turn repeated issues into actionable prompt, tool, and workflow improvements.

### Connect your agent

Start with the Currai integration skill:

```bash
npx skills add https://github.com/curraiapp/skills --skill currai
```

Follow the [setup guide](https://www.currai.app/docs/getting-started)
to configure your credentials and capture your first interaction.

Already instrumented? Currai also accepts OpenTelemetry traces
and Langfuse-compatible ingestion.
